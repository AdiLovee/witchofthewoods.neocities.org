About Me

My name is Adinah Matthews, I am 30 years old, a diviner, and an eclectical pagan witch. I've been practicing since I was 13 years old, although I wasnt able to practice much as a kid in a Catholic household, obviously. Witchcraft is only okay if it's Jesus witchcraft, after all.

My Beliefs

I have fairly typical universalist beliefs. There's a Divine energy outside of the physical plane, and all of nature is an expression of the Divine. The Divine presents itself to us through different aspects, which is why every culture that has interacted with the Divine tends towards recurring beliefs and customs around each of their deities. However, I'm not truly polytheistic, I don't pray to each aspect as a unique deity. I believe in a single deity, the Divine, and I invoke certain aspects of it through magick.

My Pantheon

Currently, the aspects I follow are, in order of devotion:
  • The Morrigan

      • My triple goddess, and my aspect of war and the cycles of life and death.

  • Freyja

      • My patron of magick, the guide for my divination, and my aspect of love and beauty.

  • Brigid

      • Solar deity, as well as my aspect of healing and handicrafts.

  • Selene

      • Lunar deity, as well as my aspect of heavenly cycles.

  • Demeter

      • My aspect of harvest and agriculture, as well as earthly cycles.

  • Athena

      • My guide for earthly matters, and my aspect of wisdom, as well as artistic crafts.
